EKF / SIH heading comparison, prepared 2026-10-06 (KST)

Scope
20 saved driving windows from 2026-10-03 through 2026-10-06 03:32,
plus three older diagnostic captures. This is the available preserved
extract coverage, not a claim to cover every historical drive.
No live tlog, hardware, ROS node, controller source or configuration was changed.

Definition and pairing
Error = wrap(EKF NED yaw - SIH NED yaw) in degrees, in [-180, 180].
EKF: MAVLink ATTITUDE.yaw. SIH: HIL_STATE_QUATERNION quaternion [w,x,y,z].
These native MAVLink quaternions are distinct from the project's ROS messages
that sometimes encode scalar yaw in orientation.z.
No fitted heading offset or fitted time lag is removed.
SIH heading is unwrapped and interpolated onto ATTITUDE receive timestamps.
Extrapolation, SIH brackets wider than 150 ms and nearest sample distance
greater than 75 ms are excluded. HIL_STATE internal time_usec is zero in
these extracts: receive-time pairing cannot eliminate transport delay.
Largest run-specific 95th-percentile nearest sample distance is 20.44 ms.
Only source (1,1) is used when source metadata is present. Nonfinite values,
invalid quaternion norms and duplicate timestamps are excluded.
The October 2 capture contains already-paired values; its pairing distance
is filtered to <=75 ms and its error sign is checked against the two headings.

Drive statistics
Start/end: first/last positive throttle COMMAND_LONG 187 in each group.
Groups are separated by >8 seconds and require >=20 commands and >3 seconds.
Within each window, statistics use SIH horizontal speed >0.5 m/s.
Prestart stationary statistics use the preceding 5 seconds with speed <0.3 m/s.
Dates in figures are actual first positive throttle times, not bag/launch times.
Timeline panels include the preceding 5 seconds and use a common vertical scale.
Different routes, speeds and configurations are included; these are not
matched-scenario controller performance comparisons.

Observed facts
- October 5 first run after reboot (23:41 launch, 23:42:31 throttle start):
  stationary prestart median -7.57 deg; moving minimum about -12.07 deg;
  moving absolute-error P95 11.99 deg, then heading discrepancy converges.
- Other 19 driving windows: absolute-error P95 ranges from 0.97 to 3.38 deg.
- October 6 01:41 and 01:43 prestart stationary medians are -0.82 and
  -1.36 deg; moving medians are +0.15 and +0.65 deg. Earlier startup readings
  and these moving statistics describe different intervals.
- Older separate captures: September 23 before reboot median +96.54 deg;
  after reboot median +0.78 deg; October 2 stationary median -10.91 deg.
  The older diagnostic figure explicitly uses different vertical scales.
These observations do not establish the cause of the estimator discrepancy
or attribute steering oscillation to heading error.
